About this module

A small but realistic module used to showcase python_beautifier. It models a warehouse that accepts orders, reserves stock, prices the basket and ships parcels.

The module is organised in four layers:

  • domain types (OrderStatus, LineItem, Order)
  • an Inventory that tracks stock levels
  • pricing helpers such as price_basket
  • an OrderProcessor that orchestrates everything
Example
>>> inv = Inventory({"ABC-0001": 10})>>> inv.reserve("ABC-0001", 3)True
